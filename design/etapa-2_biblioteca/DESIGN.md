Design System
Brand & Style

Volt Precision Warm is a soft, warm dark theme for a workout-logging app used in the gym. It feels like a gym at night: comfortable on the eyes, welcoming and trustworthy, never aggressive or like a technical dashboard. It is always dark; never use a light background. Avoid heavy gradients, avoid uppercase labels and keep screens uncluttered, with one clear primary action per screen placed in the thumb zone (bottom of the screen).

Colors
Background (#1A1715): warm graphite, never pure black. Cards use #23201D and #2D2925; inputs use #38332F. Borders are subtle (#3A3531, 1px) and used sparingly.
Primary (#FF7A59): warm coral for primary actions, active tab, selected chips and progress. Text on coral is #1A1715. Pressed state #E8654A. A soft coral tint (#3A251F) is used for highlighted cards such as the rest timer.
Tertiary (#FFC46B): amber, reserved ONLY for personal records (PR) and achievements.
Text: #F5EFE8 primary, #B7ADA3 secondary, #7D746B muted.
Status: success #7BC98F, error #FF6B6B.
Never use blue or burnt orange.
Typography

Outfit (600/700) for titles and all numbers (weight, reps, timers, totals), with tabular figures so numbers align. DM Sans (400/500) for body text and labels. Use sentence case everywhere; do not use all-caps for labels, buttons or table headers. Numbers for load and reps are large and easy to read at a glance.

Layout & Spacing

Mobile first at 390px wide, 4px base grid. Side margin 20px, gap between cards 12px, card padding 16 to 20px. Touch targets are at least 48px. The main data on each screen is the largest element on it.

Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from tonal layers (#1A1715 to #23201D to #2D2925) and very subtle shadows. Bottom sheets and modals sit on a 60 to 70 percent black overlay.

Shapes

Very rounded and friendly. Cards 24px, inputs 16px, bottom sheets 28px at the top. Buttons and chips are fully pill-shaped. The primary button is a 56px tall full-width coral pill (64px for the main action in an active workout).

Components
Primary button: coral pill, dark text, no uppercase. Secondary button: pill on #2D2925. Danger: soft red.
Tab bar: 4 tabs (Início, Treinos, Biblioteca, Progresso), 72px, active tab has a filled icon and coral label.
Chips and segmented controls: pill shaped, selected state in coral.
Steppers: round minus and plus buttons around a large Outfit number.
Icons: rounded stroke, 1.75px, friendly and consistent (Lucide style).
PR badge: amber trophy chip.
